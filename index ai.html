    How it works

    • Each student is a record of 4 features (a list) with a final mark to learn from.
    • The model finds weights so that predicted marks stay closest to actual marks (least squares).
    • 24 students are used for training and 6 for testing, so the accuracy shown is on unseen data.
    • Risk: High below 40, Medium 40 to 54, Low 55 and above.
